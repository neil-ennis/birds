About the archive

Thanks for checking out my birdsong archive.

Most days I wake to the music of our beautiful feathered friends drifting through my window. I think it’s a delightful way to start the day, and the songs seem to change with the seasons.

Apart from familiar favourites such as magpies, butcherbirds and rainbow lorikeets, I couldn’t put a name to many of the songs I heard. That changed when I started using BirdNET Live, a clever app that identifies birds by sound. BirdNET is a collaboration between the Cornell Lab of Ornithology’s K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation Bioacoustics and Chemnitz University of Technology.

The pages on this site are based on data from that app.

If you enjoy exploring the archive, please leave a comment below to let me know what you think.

To send me a message, visit the archive’s contact page.

Neil
